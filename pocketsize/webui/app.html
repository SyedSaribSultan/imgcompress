We compare the two images the way eyes do — looking at local contrast and detail rather than counting pixel differences. 100 means indistinguishable. At 90 the difference does not show even when the two are flicked back and forth on top of each other.

Colour is never thrown away, which most JPEG encoders do to save space. Matching this quality with colour detail discarded needed setting 97 instead of 76 — a file 3.8× larger.
